✨ Understanding Authentic Ruqyah Shari'yah

Ruqyah (الرقية الشرعية) is the Islamic practice of reciting verses from the Glorious Quran and authentic supplications taught by Prophet Muhammad ﷺ to seek spiritual healing, divine solace, and protection from evil whispers, harm, envy ('ayn), and witchcraft (sihr).

“And We send down of the Quran that which is healing and mercy for the believers...” — Surah Al-Isra (17:82)

1. Solely from Quran & Sunnah

Authentic Ruqyah must consist strictly of Allah’s Words, His Names and Attributes, or supplications established in authentic Sunnah without any additions.

2. Clear Arabic / Understood Meaning

Recitation must be in clear Arabic or an understandable language, entirely free from mystical symbols, amulets, knotted strings, or incomprehensible words.

3. Absolute Reliance on Allah

The reciter and the listener must firmly believe that the words have no independent healing power — cure (Shifa) descends solely from Allah.

⚠️ Scholarly Guidance & Medical Disclaimer: Seeking Quranic Ruqyah is a noble Sunnah that should accompany, rather than replace, qualified medical diagnosis and treatment. The spiritual purposes highlighted here are drawn from classical scholarly tafsir and Ruqyah compilations.
