Privacy Policy, Storage Transparency & Sources

📅 September 28, 2026 🛡️ Transparency ⏱ 4 min read
✨ The Core Guarantee: HeyNuo is an educational, non-commercial digital knowledge hub. There are no trackers, no analytics scripts, no advertising networks, and no selling or sharing of user information.

1. Zero Third-Party Trackers Commitment

Unlike modern commercial websites that run dozens of background trackers and third-party advertising scripts, HeyNuo runs pure static HTML, CSS, and lightweight Vanilla JavaScript. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixels, marketing cookies, or fingerprinting scripts. Your reading habits, article views, and listening sessions remain entirely private to your device.

2. Browser Local Storage Disclosures

To offer convenient client-side features without creating server-side accounts or cookies, this website utilizes your browser's native localStorage API. Everything is stored strictly inside your local browser and never transmitted to our servers:

Key Purpose Data Type Duration
heynuo-theme Remembers your chosen color theme (light or dark mode) String ('dark' / 'light') Persistent
heynuo-bookmarks Saves your favorited tutorial and research articles JSON Array of URLs & titles Persistent
heynuo_contact_draft Prevents message loss if your browser reloads while drafting an inquiry JSON Object (name, email, draft text) Until submitted/cleared
ruqyah_favorites Remembers your starred healing verses in the Ruqyah Guide JSON Array of verse IDs Persistent
ruqyah_reciter Remembers your selected recitation voice for Ruqyah passages String (e.g. 'ashiq') Persistent
qp_track_index, qp_volume, qp_favorites Saves Quran Audio Player state (current Surah, volume, playlist) Numbers & Arrays Persistent
heynuo_shortcuts_enabled Stores whether single-key keyboard shortcuts are enabled (WCAG 2.1.4) Boolean Persistent

3. External Audio Hosts & Network CDNs

To deliver high-fidelity Quran recitations and fluid typography with instant performance, this static site streams assets from carefully selected educational and public platforms:

  • Al-Islam Quran Cloud (files.alislam.cloud): Streams verse-by-verse Tilawat for the Ruqyah Guide recited by Qari Muhammad Ashiq, Qari Feroz, Qari Rashid, and Qari Aiman.
  • EveryAyah CDN (everyayah.com): Provides secondary fallback audio streams (Sheikh Mishary Rashid Alafasy) ensuring audio never fails silently.
  • MP3Quran (mp3quran.net) & QuranicAudio: Serves full continuous Surah recitations for the complete 114 Surahs player.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com): Serves modern web typography (Plus Jakarta Sans, Inter, Amiri) using preconnected non-blocking hints.
  • GitHub Pages: Hosts the static repository files directly from GitHub's global content delivery network.

4. Scholarly Sources & Theological Methodology

Quranic passages and Hadith citations presented on this website are compiled strictly from authentic, recognized Islamic reference works:

  • Quranic Text & Calligraphy: Standard Madinah Uthmani script adhering to the verified printing standards of the King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex in Al-Madinah Al-Munawwarah.
  • English Translations: Primarily based on the Sahih International translation, renowned for fidelity and clarity.
  • Hadith Collections: Every spiritual remedy and theological claim references classical verified Hadith canons, prominently Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Sunan Abi Dawud, and Jami` at-Tirmidhi.
  • Classical Exegesis: Interpretations and context are informed by classical Tafsir scholars, including Ibn Kathir, Al-Baghawi, and Al-Qurtubi.

5. Scholarly Guidance & Medical Disclaimer

Seeking Quranic Ruqyah is an authentic Prophetic Sunnah for spiritual solace, cure, and protection. However, Islamic scholarship universally emphasizes that seeking Ruqyah should accompany, rather than replace, qualified professional medical consultation, psychiatric care, and clinical diagnosis. Prophet Muhammad ﷺ commanded: “Make use of medical treatment, for Allah has not made a disease without appointing a remedy for it...” (Sunan Abi Dawud 3855).

The software, design system, and code architecture of this website are released under the open-source MIT License. Quranic texts, translations, and recitation audio belong to the public domain and educational Islamic waqf commons for free non-commercial learning and Tadabbur.
