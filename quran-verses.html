📖 Background, Methodology & Key Statistics (From Publication)

Analysis & Deduplication: This document presents a master compilation of 108 unique Quranic verse references extracted through the systematic analysis of 62 books spanning Islamic sciences, Ruqyah and healing traditions, Seerah (prophetic biography), Tafsir (Quranic exegesis), and Islamic history. Each entry has been deduplicated so that no verse reference appears more than once, providing a clean, authoritative reference covering 44 chapters of the Holy Quran.

Note on Source Books: Of the 62 books in the analyzed collection, approximately 30 are Western occult and non-Islamic texts containing zero Quranic content. The remaining 17–18 Islamic books yielded all 108 unique verse references documented here. The two primary contributing books are Understanding Ruqyah and Jinn in Islam (contributing the vast majority of verses) and Ism-e-Azam (Ilmul Hisab) (contributing 4 specialized verses connected to Prophetic names of Allah).
